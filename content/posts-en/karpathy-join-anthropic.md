Yesterday, Karpathy announced he is joining Anthropic on the pretraining team. An OpenAI co-founder, former Director of AI at Tesla, and the person who coined Vibe Coding—his new title is Member of Technical Staff, literally just a technical staff member.

He is not the first to do this.

## One Person Is a Job Change; a Group Is a Signal

Over the past 18 months, a wave of accomplished technical leaders have made the same choice: giving up titles like CTO, founder, and chief scientist to join frontier AI labs as MTS.

Mike Krieger, who built Instagram from zero to a billion monthly active users, voluntarily stepped down from CPO to MTS after joining Anthropic to lead Claude Code. Ruoming Pang left his role heading Apple's foundation models team to take an MTS role at OpenAI as well.

SignalFire's 2025 data is even blunter: OpenAI engineers are 8x more likely to leave for Anthropic than the reverse, and the ratio from DeepMind to Anthropic is nearly 11 to 1. Talent is flowing in one direction.

## What Are They Actually Giving Up?

At OpenAI and Anthropic, MTS is a unified title borrowed from Bell Labs: regardless of seniority, and without distinguishing between research and engineering, everyone is a Member of Technical Staff. Teams still exist, and collaboration still happens. What these leaders are actually giving up is the indirect leverage of management hierarchy—reporting lines, budget sign-off, and a box at the top of the org chart.

In the traditional software era, a CTO's power came from the organization: headcount, budget control, and approval gates. In the LLM era, a CTO manages ten thousand people and sits three reporting layers away from training decisions; an MTS manages zero people and directly shapes model architecture choices.

## Hands-On Feel Beats a Title

**Titles are inflating; hands-on intuition is scarce.**

These people chose to "downgrade" after achieving financial freedom because what holds value has shifted from "how many people you manage" to "whether you can get your hands on the model." Karpathy picking the pretraining team over a VP title is the answer in itself.
