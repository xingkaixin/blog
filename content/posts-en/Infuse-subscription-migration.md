If you use Plex to manage your movies and TV shows, Infuse delivers the best viewing experience on iPhone and Apple TV. To unlock every feature and keep getting future updates, most people choose an in-app subscription.

I subscribed to Infuse a while back, but I hadn't looked closely enough at regional availability—since the China region doesn't have an Apple TV App Store, I couldn't use it on my Apple TV. A couple of days ago, I emailed Infuse support asking if they could transfer my Chinese-region subscription to my Hong Kong account. After confirming my order details, they simply sent me a promo code to redeem. As a result, I now have active subscriptions in both the China and Hong Kong regions at the same time—and the Hong Kong subscription starts from the date I redeemed the code, including a free bonus month.

Great experience, great product.
