On the last day before Chinese New Year, the NiceTry podcast dropped two episodes in a row. In one of them, cbvivi mentioned _Ted Lasso_ on Apple TV+, and from the way he described it, the show sounded pretty fun. Since Apple TV+ launched, I have only watched _The Morning Show_ and _Greyhound_. With the other shows, I either dropped them after a few minutes or just watched online plot recaps—nothing really motivated me to follow them.

_Ted Lasso_ has 10 episodes, 30 minutes each—probably around 20-something minutes without the end credits—so I finished the whole thing in one afternoon. The pacing is very fast; one season covers an entire football season. My first impression was that it felt like an earnest, upbeat Japanese drama: you start out with just one ally while everyone else is an enemy, win them over one by one, and end up with a happy reunion. Watching through to the end, the story really does follow that exact arc. Ted is an optimistic idealist—no matter how others treat him, he treats them even better.

So in terms of plot, it is pretty formulaic. Still, it has plenty of great jokes, short episodes, and brisk pacing, so I definitely recommend watching it.

The show is now ordered for three seasons, and the next one should be out in the second half of this year. I am really looking forward to it.
