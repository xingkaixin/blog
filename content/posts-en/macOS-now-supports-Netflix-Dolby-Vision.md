At last year's WWDC 19, I thought macOS was getting 4K support, but after several betas, it became clear that wasn't going to happen.

In the recent 10.15.4 beta 5, I noticed that Netflix in Safari can now trigger the Dolby Vision badge. After asking around, I confirmed it works on 2018 or newer Mac models running beta 5. It looks like 10.15 is finally getting this feature—I wonder how much longer we'll have to wait for 4K.
